**Owner assessment: none.** Quan stated that nothing went well in this epic. The work is merged but not released, so users have received no value.

## Challenges

The Owner confirmed all of these as problems.

1. **Built but not released.** No story or rule required Epic 14 to reach `main`. Epics 12 and 13 had release stories (12.11, 13.13); Epic 14 did not.
2. **Tasks checked [x] without the tests.** 14.1 (race tests, HTTP e2e, CHECK tests missing), 14.3 (weak assertions), 14.5 (prettier failed on 12 files, atomicity test stubbed the outbox, 2 tests missing). Second occurrence after 13.12.
3. **CI gates missing from stories.** `contract-callsites` was not listed; 14.2 failed it, and 14.3–14.5 worked around it.
4. **"FE only" scope was wrong in 4 of 5 stories.** 14.2 (BE e2e), 14.3 (read DTO), 14.4 (drift query, audit appends), 14.5 (audit repo, enum migration).
5. **Pre-existing failures add noise to every story.** `rewards-flash.prisma.e2e:144`, the clock-dependent `rewards-admin-flash-domain` "stats: daily" test, prettier on the missing-code spec. The wallet e2e count was fixed in `718f2ff`.
6. **Tracking drift.** `sprint-status` comments for 14.1 and 14.5 said "uncommitted" after merge. 14.2 was merged before review.
7. **Audit visibility gap.** 14.3 D1=A shows `identity_audit_logs` only; Outbox `admin-audit:*` events (top-up, moderation, missing-code, voucher) are invisible.

## Key Insights

- "Done" meant "merged to `develop`". For users, done means "on production".
- Test evidence must be recorded, not asserted by a checkbox.
- Story templates must list every CI gate the change will meet.
