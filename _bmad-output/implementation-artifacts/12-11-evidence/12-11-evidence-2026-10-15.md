## 7. Defect found and fixed during this run

Defect: `apps/backend/src/modules/rewards/application/rewards-redemption.service.ts:254` passed `{ ...voucher, codesAvailable: voucher.stockRemaining }` to `decideRedemption`. Flash reservation moves the whole quota out of `stock_remaining`, so when a Flash Sale reserves all remaining stock (quota = stock) `stockRemaining` is 0, `voucherAvailability` returned `SOLD_OUT`, and `effectiveStatus` kept it `SOLD_OUT` because `codesAvailable > 0` was false. Every valid Flash redeem was rejected with `SOLD_OUT` and the buyer was not charged; quotes succeeded because they use the real code count. It was reproduced by the journey step "Flash where quota = whole stock" and by the load test at the specified size, and no earlier PG suite covered quota = whole stock.

Fix (applied by the lead, not by this run): the decision now receives `codesAvailable: Math.max(voucher.codesAvailable, voucher.stockRemaining)`, keeping the 2.h code-claim drift check on the regular path, plus a new PG test "quota equal to the whole stock" in `test/rewards-flash.prisma.e2e-spec.ts`. Verified here by rebuilding the image and rerunning the journey (216/216), the flag-off sweep (19/19) and the load test (100/100, losers `SOLD_OUT`).
