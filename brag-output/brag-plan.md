# Rescom brag plan

**What:** Rescom is a survey marketplace for Vietnamese university students. You answer other students' surveys to earn points, then spend those points to get answers for your own survey.
**For:** students who need survey data for theses and coursework (publishers) and students with a few spare minutes (respondents).
**Edge:** a fair point economy ("Nhận hỗ trợ — Đóng góp lại"), so you stop begging the class group chat for responses.
**Hook:** the familiar class-chat plea, "Làm giúp mình khảo sát với ạ 🙏", repeated until the screen fills up.
**Tone:** default (playful, clean), in the brand's own green and Be Vietnam Pro, with the Fraunces headline from the landing page.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 21s. Music at 120 BPM (one bar is 2s), with scene cuts on the beat.

## Storyboard

| # | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3.0 | Hook | Chat bubbles stack up fast: "Mọi người ơi làm giúp mình khảo sát với ạ 🙏". Big type at the end: "Cần 100 câu trả lời?" |
| 2 | 3.0–6.0 | Reveal | Real landing hero (Fraunces headline + hills), slow push-in. Logo. |
| 3 | 6.0–10.0 | Earn | Real Khám phá marketplace. Camera pushes into the "+12 điểm" card, a cursor clicks "Bắt đầu", a +12 chip flies off, and a counter ticks up. Caption: "Làm khảo sát. Nhận điểm." |
| 4 | 10.0–14.0 | Spend | Real "Khảo sát của tôi" page: progress 12/30, then a slide to the real response donut. Caption: "Dùng điểm. Nhận câu trả lời thật." |
| 5 | 14.0–17.5 | Community | Real leaderboard slides in, with the trophy mascot. Caption: "Cả cộng đồng sinh viên cùng góp sức." |
| 6 | 17.5–21.0 | Outro | Cheer mascot, Rescom logo, "Nhận hỗ trợ — Đóng góp lại." |

Sound: a soft C-major house loop (kick, hats, plucked chords C–Am–F–G, bass). The pops are tuned to the key and the whooshes are filtered noise, kept under the music.
